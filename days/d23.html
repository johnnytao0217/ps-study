📘 今日新知

电动势 emf (ε)：电池没有电流时两端的电压。电池内部有内阻 r，有电流时一部分电压“损失”在内阻上：lost volts = Ir。

端电压 V = ε − Ir。外电阻越小 → 电流越大 → lost volts 越大 → 端电压越低。完整公式：ε = I(R + r)，R 是外电路总电阻。

V-I 图：直线的 y 截距 = ε，斜率 = −r。

功率 P = W/Δt = VI = I²R = V²/R；能量 W = PΔt。电费：kW × h = kWh，再乘以单价。

🧮 公式

ε = I(R + r)V = ε − IrP = VI = I²R = V²RW = PΔt1 kWh = 3,6 × 10⁶ J

Emf · 电动势
The maximum energy provided by a battery per coulomb of charge passing through it (the potential difference across the battery when no current flows).
Power · 功率
The rate at which work is done (or energy is transferred).
Internal resistance · 内阻
The resistance within a battery that causes a drop in potential difference (lost volts) when current flows.
