📘 今日新知

物体带电是因为电子转移：得到电子带负电，失去电子带正电（质子不移动）。电荷量是电子电荷的整数倍：Q = n·e，e = 1,6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C。

电荷守恒：孤立系统总电荷不变。两个相同的导体球接触后分开：各自得到 (Q₁ + Q₂)/2。

库仑定律：F = kQ₁Q₂/r²。代入时只代大小（不带正负号），然后用文字说明是吸引（异号）还是排斥（同号）。

三个电荷在一条直线上：分别求另外两个电荷对它的力，按方向相加减。

🧮 公式

Q = n·eQnew = Q₁ + Q₂2F = kQ₁Q₂r²k = 9,0 × 10⁹ N·m²·C⁻²

Principle of conservation of charge · 电荷守恒
The net charge of an isolated system remains constant during any physical process.
Principle of charge quantization · 电荷量子化
Every charge is an integer multiple of the charge of an electron (e = 1,6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C).
Coulomb’s Law · 库仑定律
The magnitude of the electrostatic force exerted by one point charge on another is directly proportional to the product of the magnitudes of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.
