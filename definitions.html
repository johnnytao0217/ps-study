术语定义（考试英文原句风格）✓
Scalar
标量		A physical quantity that has magnitude only.
Vector
矢量		A physical quantity that has both magnitude and direction.
Resultant vector
合矢量		The single vector that has the same effect as all the original vectors acting together.
Displacement
位移		The change in position of an object (a vector from initial to final position).
Speed
速率		The rate of change of distance.
Velocity
速度		The rate of change of position (displacement).
Acceleration
加速度		The rate of change of velocity.
Uniform acceleration
匀加速		Acceleration that remains constant in magnitude and direction.
Instantaneous velocity
瞬时速度		The velocity of an object at a particular moment in time (the gradient of the x-t graph at that instant).
Newton’s First Law
牛顿第一定律		An object continues in a state of rest or uniform motion (constant velocity) unless it is acted upon by a net (resultant) force.
Newton’s Second Law
牛顿第二定律		When a net force acts on an object, the object accelerates in the direction of the net force. The acceleration is directly proportional to the net force and inversely proportional to the mass of the object.
Inertia
惯性		The property of an object that causes it to resist a change in its state of rest or uniform motion.
Normal force
法向力		The force exerted by a surface on an object in contact with it, acting perpendicular to the surface.
Kinetic friction
动摩擦力		The force that opposes the motion of a moving object relative to a surface.
Static friction
静摩擦力		The force that opposes the tendency of motion of a stationary object relative to a surface.
Newton’s Third Law
牛顿第三定律		When object A exerts a force on object B, object B simultaneously exerts an oppositely directed force of equal magnitude on object A.
Newton’s Law of Universal Gravitation
万有引力定律		Every particle with mass in the universe attracts every other particle with a force which is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between their centres.
Weight
重力		The gravitational force the Earth exerts on any object on or near its surface.
Principle of conservation of charge
电荷守恒		The net charge of an isolated system remains constant during any physical process.
Principle of charge quantization
电荷量子化		Every charge is an integer multiple of the charge of an electron (e = 1,6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C).
Coulomb’s Law
库仑定律		The magnitude of the electrostatic force exerted by one point charge on another is directly proportional to the product of the magnitudes of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.
Electric field
电场		A region of space in which an electric charge experiences a force.
Electric field at a point
某点的电场强度		The electrostatic force experienced per unit positive charge placed at that point.
Current
电流		The rate of flow of charge.
Potential difference
电势差		The work done per unit positive charge to move it between two points.
Resistance
电阻		The opposition to the flow of electric current (ratio of potential difference to current).
Ohm’s Law
欧姆定律		The potential difference across a conductor is directly proportional to the current in the conductor at constant temperature.
Emf
电动势		The maximum energy provided by a battery per coulomb of charge passing through it (the potential difference across the battery when no current flows).
Power
功率		The rate at which work is done (or energy is transferred).
Internal resistance
内阻		The resistance within a battery that causes a drop in potential difference (lost volts) when current flows.

术语定义（考试英文原句风格）✓
Covalent bond
共价键		The sharing of electrons between two atoms to form a molecule.
Electronegativity
电负性		A measure of the tendency of an atom in a molecule to attract bonding electrons.
Dative covalent bond
配位键		A covalent bond in which both shared electrons are provided by the same atom.
Bond length
键长		The average distance between the nuclei of two bonded atoms.
Bond energy
键能		The energy needed to break one mole of a particular bond (to separate the atoms).
Molecular shape (VSEPR)
分子形状		The shape is determined by the repulsion between electron pairs (bonding and lone pairs) around the central atom, which arrange themselves as far apart as possible.
Polar molecule
极性分子		A molecule with an uneven distribution of charge, so that one end is slightly positive and the other slightly negative.
Intermolecular forces
分子间作用力		Forces of attraction between molecules (or between molecules and ions).
Hydrogen bond
氢键		A strong dipole-dipole force between a hydrogen atom bonded to a small, highly electronegative atom (N, O or F) and a lone pair on an N, O or F atom of another molecule.
London (dispersion) forces
London 力		Weak forces between molecules caused by temporary (induced) dipoles.
Boiling point
沸点		The temperature at which the vapour pressure of a liquid equals the atmospheric (external) pressure.
Vapour pressure
蒸气压		The pressure exerted by a vapour in equilibrium with its liquid in a closed system.
Melting point
熔点		The temperature at which the solid and liquid phases of a substance are in equilibrium.
Boyle’s law
波义耳定律		The pressure of an enclosed gas is inversely proportional to its volume, provided the temperature and amount of gas remain constant.
Charles’ law
查理定律		The volume of an enclosed gas is directly proportional to its Kelvin temperature, provided the pressure and amount of gas remain constant.
Gay-Lussac’s law
盖-吕萨克定律		The pressure of an enclosed gas is directly proportional to its Kelvin temperature, provided the volume and amount of gas remain constant.
Absolute zero
绝对零度		The temperature (0 K = −273 °C) at which particles have minimum kinetic energy.
Ideal gas
理想气体		A gas whose particles have no volume and no intermolecular forces, and which obeys the gas laws at all temperatures and pressures.
Exothermic reaction
放热反应		A reaction that releases energy to the surroundings (ΔH < 0).
Endothermic reaction
吸热反应		A reaction that absorbs energy from the surroundings (ΔH > 0).
Activation energy
活化能		The minimum energy needed for a reaction to take place.
Activated complex
活化络合物		The unstable transition state with the highest potential energy during a reaction.
Catalyst
催化剂		A substance that increases the rate of a reaction but remains unchanged at the end of the reaction.
Mole
摩尔		The SI unit for amount of substance; one mole contains as many particles as there are atoms in 12 g of carbon-12.
Molar mass
摩尔质量		The mass of one mole of a substance (g·mol⁻¹).
Molar concentration
物质的量浓度		The amount of solute per unit volume of solution (mol·dm⁻³).
Molar gas volume
摩尔气体体积		The volume occupied by one mole of any gas (22,4 dm³ at STP).
Empirical formula
经验式		The simplest whole-number ratio of atoms of each element in a compound.
Molecular formula
分子式		The actual number of atoms of each element in one molecule of a compound.
Limiting reagent
限量试剂		The reactant that is completely used up in a reaction and therefore limits the amount of product formed.
Percentage yield
产率		The actual yield expressed as a percentage of the theoretical yield.
Percentage purity
纯度		The mass of the pure substance expressed as a percentage of the mass of the impure sample.
Homologous series
同系物		A series of organic compounds that can be described by the same general formula and in which each member differs from the next by a CH₂ group.
Saturated compound
饱和化合物		A compound in which there are only single bonds between carbon atoms.
Unsaturated compound
不饱和化合物		A compound with one or more multiple bonds (double or triple) between carbon atoms.
Functional group
官能团		A bond or an atom or a group of atoms that determines the physical and chemical properties of a group of organic compounds.
Structural isomers
结构异构体		Organic molecules with the same molecular formula but different structural formulae.
